Assumptions

  • Hours are focused development hours by one person, already assuming AI-assisted coding. Meetings with the client are not counted.

  • Estimates exclude Figma polish beyond the core screens, content writing for the Homework Bank, and store-review waiting time (plan 1 to 2 weeks per release).
