Telegram leads

People who registered interest through the centre’s Telegram bot land here before they become students.

Review registrations from the Telegram bot LED-review-leads

/telegram-registrations CRUD_STUDENT · sidebar: VIEW_TELEGRAM_LEADS
  1. Open Telegram Leads from the sidebar.
  2. Each row is one registration, with the name and contact details the person entered in the bot.

Refresh the leads list LED-refresh-leads

/telegram-registrations CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Click Refresh at the top of the page to pull in any registrations submitted since the page loaded.

Assign a lead to a group and teacher LED-assign-lead

/telegram-registrations CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Find the lead and click its assign action.
  2. Choose the class (and therefore teacher) to enrol them into.
  3. Click Confirm & Move — the lead becomes an enrolled student and disappears from this list.

Reject a lead LED-reject-lead

/telegram-registrations CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Find the lead and click Reject.
  2. Confirm — the registration is closed and no student account is created.
