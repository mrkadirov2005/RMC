Student profile

Every student has a profile page with tabs for their day-to-day records. Open it from the Students list by clicking a student’s name or the row’s View action.

Open a student’s profile SPR-open-profile

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. From Students, click the student’s row.
  2. The profile opens on the Overview tab, with Attendance, Payments, Assignments, Individual Tasks, Grades and Coins tabs along the top.

Edit a student SPR-edit-student

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. On the student’s profile, click Edit information (or Edit from the Students list row menu).
  2. Change any field — the same fields as adding a student — and save.

Edit the overview inline SPR-edit-overview-inline

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. On the Overview tab, click Edit information.
  2. Update the fields shown and click Save without leaving the profile page.

Activate or deactivate a student SPR-toggle-status

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. On the student’s profile, open the status control near the top of the Overview tab.
  2. Choose the new status and confirm — the change applies immediately.

Set a student’s password SPR-set-password-profile

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. On the profile, click Update Password.
  2. Type the new password, or generate one, then save.

Reset a student’s password and copy it SPR-reset-password-profile

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. On the profile, click Reset Password.
  2. A new password is generated and shown once — click Copy to copy it before closing.
  3. Click Done to close the dialog. The password cannot be shown again after this.

Add, edit or delete attendance records SPR-attendance-tab

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the student’s profile and click the Attendance tab.
  2. Click Add Record to log a new entry, or the row’s edit icon to change one.
  3. Click the row’s delete icon and confirm to remove a record.

Add, edit or delete payments SPR-payments-tab

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the student’s profile and click the Payments tab.
  2. Click Add Payment to record one, or the row’s edit icon to change one.
  3. Use the row’s delete icon and confirm to remove a payment.

Add, edit or delete assignments SPR-assignments-tab-profile

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the student’s profile and click the Assignments tab.
  2. Click Add Assignment, fill in the title, description and due date, and save.
  3. Click a row to edit it, or Cancel to close without saving.

Add, edit or delete individual tasks SPR-individual-tasks-tab

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the student’s profile and click the Individual Tasks tab.
  2. Use the same Add, edit and delete controls as the Assignments tab — these tasks apply to this one student only.

Add, edit or delete grades SPR-grades-tab

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the student’s profile and click the Grades tab.
  2. Click Add Grade, fill in the score, and save.
  3. Click a row to edit it, or use its delete icon to remove it.

Give or take coins SPR-coins-tab

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the student’s profile and click the Coins tab.
  2. Click Update Coins.
  3. Enter the amount to add or remove and a short reason, then save.

Delete a coin transaction SPR-delete-coin-tx

/students/:studentId CRUD_STUDENT
  1. On the Coins tab, find the transaction in the history.
  2. Click its delete icon and confirm — the balance recalculates automatically.
