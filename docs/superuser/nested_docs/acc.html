Account and access

Every branch admin signs in at their own page, separate from the owner, teacher and student logins.

Sign in as superuser ACC-sign-in-superuser

/login/superuser
  1. Go to /login/superuser (the login screen also has an Admin link from the other role logins).
  2. Type your Username and Password.
  3. Tick Remember me to stay signed in longer, or leave it unticked for a session-only login.
  4. Click Continue to Admin.
  5. You land on the Dashboard.
Form fields
  • Username
  • Password
  • Remember mecheckbox

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page ACC-forgot-password-su

/login/superuser
  1. On the sign-in form, click Forgot password?.
  2. A notice appears: “Please contact Temurbek School administration to reset your access.” No reset email is sent.

Open a page without permission ACC-unauthorized-page

/unauthorized
  1. Trying to open a page you don’t have permission for redirects here.
  2. The page explains access is missing.

Return to the dashboard from an error page ACC-go-to-dashboard-error

  1. On the Unauthorized page (or a missing page), click Go to Dashboard to leave it.

Reorder the sidebar ACC-reorder-sidebar-admin

  1. Click and hold a sidebar item, then drag it up or down.
  2. Release to drop it in the new position — the order is remembered.

Switch between light and dark theme ACC-theme-toggle-admin

  1. Click the theme button near the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. The whole interface switches immediately.

Sign out ACC-sign-out-admin

  1. Click Logout at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. You return to the sign-in page.
