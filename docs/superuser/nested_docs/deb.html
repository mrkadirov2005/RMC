Debts

Debts track money a student owes that has not yet been collected.

Search debts DEB-search-debts

/debts CRUD_DEBT
  1. Open Debts and type into the search box to narrow the list.

Add a debt record DEB-add-debt

/debts CRUD_DEBT
  1. Click Add Debt.
  2. Choose the Student (and Center, if you are the owner).
  3. Enter the Debt Amount and Amount Paid, then the Debt Date and Due Date.
  4. Add any Remarks and save.
Form fields
  • Student
  • Debt Amount
  • Amount Paid
  • Debt Date
  • Due Date
  • Remarksoptional

Edit or delete a debt DEB-edit-delete-debt

/debts CRUD_DEBT
  1. Click a debt row’s edit action to change it, or its delete action and confirm to remove it.

Generate debt records from unpaid months DEB-generate-debts

/debts CRUD_DEBT
  1. Open Debts and open the debt analyser panel.
  2. Click Select All, or tick individual months found to be unpaid.
  3. Click Generate Debts (n) and confirm — a debt record is created for each selected month.
