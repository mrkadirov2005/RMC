Business Rules

Validation
  • serial_discount and monthly_discount should not double-discount unless explicitly allowed.
  • If both are present, define priority. Recommended: monthly discount overrides serial discount or user confirms stacking.
  • Payment final amount must never be negative.
  • Percent discount must be between 0 and 100.
  • Fixed discount must be less than or equal to original amount.
  • Historical payment discount snapshot must stay unchanged after discount record is edited.
