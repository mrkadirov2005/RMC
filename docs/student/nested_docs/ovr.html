Portal home

The portal home is a student’s summary of everything due and everything recently done.

Move between Home, My Tests and Calendar OVR-move-between-sections

/student-portal
  1. Use the header links Overview, My Tests and Calendar to switch sections.

Read the summary cards OVR-read-summary-cards

/student-portal
  1. On the portal home, review the cards for your teacher, class, subjects, attendance, average grade and any outstanding debt.

Read today’s and this week’s schedule OVR-read-schedule

/student-portal
  1. The schedule section on the home page lists your lessons with times and rooms.

See the next test and assignment OVR-next-test-assignment

/student-portal
  1. The home page highlights whichever test or assignment is due soonest.

Read upcoming tests and assignments due OVR-upcoming-due

/student-portal
  1. Scroll the home page’s upcoming section — everything coming up is listed in order.

Read recent grades OVR-recent-grades

/student-portal
  1. The recent grades section shows your latest marks from lessons and tests.

Read payment history OVR-payment-history-student

/student-portal
  1. The payment history section on the home page lists past payments and anything outstanding.

Open my profile OVR-open-my-profile

/student-portal
  1. Click your avatar in the header, or My Profile on the home page.
  2. Your own details and coin balance are shown.

Go back to the portal from My Tests OVR-back-from-mytests

/student-portal
  1. On My Tests, click Back.
  2. The portal home opens.

Read notifications OVR-read-notifications

/student-portal
  1. Click the bell icon in the header.

Known issueThe bell button does not do anything yet.
