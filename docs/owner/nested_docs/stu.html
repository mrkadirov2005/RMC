Students list

The Students page is where every enrolled student is found, added, and organised into classes. Open it from the sidebar item “Students”.

Find a student with search and filters STU-find-student

/students
  1. Open Students from the sidebar.
  2. Type a name, phone number or ID into the search box at the top of the list — the list narrows as you type.
  3. Click Filters to open the filter bar, and set any of class, status, teacher or payment state.
  4. Click Clear All in the filter bar to remove every filter and search term at once.

Clear all student filters STU-clear-student-filters-list

/students
  1. Open Students.
  2. Click Clear All in the filter bar — the search term and every filter reset, and the full list returns.

Open a student from the list STU-open-student-from-list

/students
  1. Open Students, find the student, and open the row’s actions menu.
  2. Click View — the student’s profile opens.

Add a student STU-add-student

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students from the sidebar.
  2. Click Add Student above the list. A dialog titled “Add Student” opens.
  3. Fill in the required fields — first name, last name, phone, date of birth, parent name, parent phone, gender and status are all required. School name and school class are optional.
  4. If you are the owner, also choose the Center.
  5. Choose the student’s Teacher and Class from the dropdowns — a class must belong to the chosen teacher.
  6. Optional: tick Discount to open the discount fields, then choose Serial, One-time, Fixed or Percent and enter the value and a reason.
  7. Optional: choose where the student came from under the acquisition source dropdown (Advertisement, Teacher referral, Student or parent referral, Social media, Walk-in, Other, or add a custom source).
  8. Click Save student.
Form fields
  • First Name / Last Namerequired
  • Phonerequired
  • Date of Birthrequired
  • Parent Name / Parent Phonerequired
  • School (optional) / School Class (optional)
  • Genderrequired, select
  • Statusrequired, select
  • Teacherrequired, select
  • Classrequired, select
  • Discountoptional toggle — Serial / One-time / Fixed / Percent, value, reason

Import students from CSV STU-import-students

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students and click the file icon next to Export CSV (it opens the file picker).
  2. Choose a CSV file from your computer. Use Export CSV first on an existing list if you need to see the expected column layout.
  3. The import runs immediately after you pick the file — watch for a success or error toast.

If a row fails, fix that row in the CSV and re-import just the corrected rows — importing does not remove existing students.

Export students to CSV STU-export-students

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students, apply any filters you want reflected in the export, then click Export CSV.
  2. The file downloads with the students currently shown in the list.

Push students to Google Sheets STU-sheets-push

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students and click Update Sheets.
  2. Wait for the button to show “Updating…” and finish — the linked Google Sheet now matches the student list in the app.

This requires the centre’s Google Sheets link to already be configured; ask the owner if the button errors.

Import students from Google Sheets STU-sheets-pull

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students and click Import Sheets.
  2. Wait for “Importing…” to finish — students added or edited in the linked sheet are pulled into the app.

Transfer a student to another class STU-transfer-student

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students, find the student, and click the row’s actions menu (Open actions).
  2. Choose Transfer.
  3. Pick the destination class and confirm.

Transfer several students at once STU-bulk-transfer

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students and tick the checkbox on each student to move.
  2. In the bulk action bar that appears, click Transfer.
  3. Pick the destination class and confirm — every selected student moves together.

Delete several students at once STU-bulk-delete

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students and tick the checkbox on each student to remove.
  2. In the bulk action bar, click Delete.
  3. Confirm — the selected students move to the archive together.

Set a student’s password from the list STU-set-password-list

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the row’s actions menu on Students and choose the password option.
  2. Type the new password and save.

Delete a student STU-delete-student

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open the row’s actions menu on Students and choose Delete.
  2. Confirm in the dialog — the student moves to the archive and can be restored from there.

Read student statistics STU-student-statistics

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students and click the Statistics tab at the top of the page.
  2. Review the counts and breakdowns shown — this tab has no editable fields.

Browse groups by teacher STU-groups-by-teacher

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students and click the Teachers tab.
  2. Click a teacher to see their groups, or a group to see its students.
  3. Use Back to teachers or Back to classes to move up a level.

Move a group to another teacher STU-move-group

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students → Teachers tab, open the group to move.
  2. Click Transfer, choose the new teacher, and click Transfer in the dialog to confirm.

Delete several groups at once STU-bulk-delete-groups

/students CRUD_STUDENT
  1. Open Students → Teachers tab, tick the checkbox on each group to remove.
  2. Click Delete in the bar that appears, then confirm.
