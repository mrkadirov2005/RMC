Account and access

The owner has two extra screens branch admins never see: a keyword-locked registration page, and a separate owner sign-in.

Register the owner account ACC-register-owner

/owner/register
  1. Go to /owner/register (or click Create owner account with keyword on the owner sign-in page).
  2. On the “Unlock owner registration” step, type the shared Owner keyword and click Continue.
  3. On the “Create owner account” step, fill in First Name, Last Name, Username, Email, Password and Confirm password.
  4. Click Create Owner Account — you are signed in immediately and land on the Owner Panel.
Form fields
  • Owner keyword
  • First Name / Last Name
  • Username
  • Email
  • Password / Confirm password

The keyword step exists so daily staff accounts stay separate from system ownership; the password and confirm password must match or the form shows “Passwords do not match.”

Sign in as owner ACC-sign-in-owner

/login/owner
  1. Go to /login/owner (or click Owner from another role’s login page).
  2. Type your Username and Password, tick Remember me if wanted, and click Access Manager Panel.
  3. You land on the Owner Panel.
Form fields
  • Username
  • Password
  • Remember mecheckbox

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page ACC-forgot-password-owner

/login/owner
  1. On the owner sign-in form, click Forgot password?.
  2. A notice appears asking you to contact the school administration. No reset happens automatically.

Sign out ACC-sign-out-owner

  1. Click Logout at the bottom of the sidebar.
