Payments

Every payment a student makes is recorded here, with group and total views for teacher income.

Record a payment PAY-record-payment

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Open Payments and click Add Payment.
  2. Choose the Student — if they have an active discount, it is applied automatically and shown under Student discount.
  3. Choose the payment method and type, set the amount, Payment date, and Notes if needed.
  4. Check Final payable reflects the discount correctly, then save.
Form fields
  • Student
  • Payment method / type
  • Payment date
  • Notesoptional
  • Discount value / Final payableshown automatically when a discount is active

Edit a payment PAY-edit-payment

/payments/:paymentId/edit CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Click a payment row’s edit action on Payments.
  2. Change the details and save.

Delete a payment PAY-delete-payment

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Click a payment row’s delete action.
  2. Confirm — it moves to the archive.

Read payment history PAY-payment-history

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Open Payments and use the search box and filters to find past payments.

Open a student’s payment history PAY-open-student-payment-history

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Click a payment row, or a student’s name, to see every payment that student has made.

Import or export payments PAY-import-export-payments

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Click Export CSV to download payments, or use the import control to add many at once.

Read payments by teacher group or total PAY-payments-by-teacher

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Open Payments and click Group payments.
  2. Use Groups and Total to switch between per-group and per-teacher totals.

Read payment statistics PAY-payment-statistics

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. Open Payments and click the Statistics tab.

Change the student on a new payment PAY-clear-payment-student

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. On the Add Payment form, click Change next to the chosen student to pick a different one.
  2. This also clears any discount that was applied.

Jump to the salary report from Payments PAY-jump-to-salary

/payments CRUD_PAYMENT
  1. On Payments, click View full Salary report to open the Salary page.
