Portal and overview

The portal’s tab bar is how a teacher moves between every part of their work.

Reorder the portal tabs PRT-reorder-tabs

/teacher-portal
  1. Click and hold a tab in the portal’s tab bar and drag it to a new position.

Reset the tab order PRT-reset-tab-order

/teacher-portal
  1. Click Reset order near the tab bar to return the tabs to their default order.

Read the Overall tab PRT-read-overall

/teacher-portal
  1. Open Teacher Portal — it opens on the Overall tab by default.
  2. Student count, attendance, points and payments are shown, with a drill-down per group.

Read the Statistics tab PRT-read-statistics-teacher

/teacher-portal
  1. Click the Statistics tab.
  2. Set a From and To date, and optionally filter by student, to see charts of your classes against the whole centre.

Switch statistics between daily and monthly PRT-statistics-granularity

/teacher-portal
  1. On the Statistics tab, use the granularity switch to regroup the charts by day or by month.

Use the quick actions menu PRT-quick-actions

/teacher-portal
  1. Open the quick actions menu in the portal header.
  2. Choose Create Test to open the test builder, Take Attendance to open the Attendance tab, or Create Assignment to open the assignment form.

Known issue“Create Assignment” opens a page only superusers can use, so a teacher following it lands on Unauthorized. “Take Attendance” opens the read-only Attendance tab rather than starting a lesson.

Use the header Notifications and Schedule buttons PRT-header-buttons

/teacher-portal
  1. Click the bell or schedule icon in the portal header.
